Original AI-generated illustration created for this project with the built-in image generation tool. Fictional chibi basketball trainee with silver middle-parted hair and white suspenders. No real-person photographs are bundled.

Generation brief: a compact full-body anime boy sticker, black shirt, white suspenders, orange basketball, clean outlines, transparent background; no scenery, lettering, or photographic realism.
